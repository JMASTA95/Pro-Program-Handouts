Step-by-step protocol

Setup & preparation

  1. Connect ozone generator to oxygen tank regulator using silicone (ozone-safe) tubing.
  2. Set the generator concentration. Start at 20 Gamma (ug/ml) for the first month.
  3. Open clamp on ozone bag, turn on oxygen & generator. Fill bag to the 200 ml mark.
  4. Turn off oxygen, turn off the generator, and clamp the bag to seal the gas inside.

Administration

  1. Attach a catheter to the clamped ozone bag tube. Ensure connection is secure.
  2. Insert catheter approximately 3 inches into the rectum. Lubricant is a personal preference.
  3. Steadily roll up the bag to release gas. Remove the catheter while keeping the bag rolled.
  4. Hold gas for 15–30 seconds. A bowel movement afterward does not reduce effectiveness.
